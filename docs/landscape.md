# Forecasting procurement outcomes: who has tried, and what public data supports it

Reviewed September 2026 by the Thesis Institute. Every claim links to the source we read; where we could not open a primary source we say so. The workstreams in this repository are retrospective backtests, a first test of whether the gap below is worth filling.

## The gap

We found no system, in government, academia, commerce, or civic technology, that registers a forecast of a contract's outcome at or before award, resolves it against an official record, and publishes the score. The parts exist separately: retrospective models of cost overrun, reference class forecasting, corruption risk flags, funders that forecast their own grants, and one statutory program that compares predicted with actual results. No one has joined them into a loop.

## Nearest work

| What exists | Who | What it does | What it leaves open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probabilistic predictions written at grant decisions, scored later | [Open Philanthropy](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/RjNFyJS3jPb4DA7wA/how-accurate-are-open-phil-s-predictions): 2,850 predictions, 743 scored, Brier 0.217, overconfident above 90 percent. [GiveWell](https://blog.givewell.org/2024/11/08/making-predictions-about-our-grants/) publishes forecasts on grant pages and [looks back](https://www.givewell.org/research/lookbacks/Dispensers-for-Safe-Water-2025): 7 of 13 forecasts on one grant resolved yes against 65 percent mean confidence | Proves the workflow inside a funder | Internal, small, resolved by the funder itself |
| Statutory predicted-versus-actual studies | US Federal Transit Administration Before-and-After Studies; [GAO-23-105479](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105479) reports 86 percent of projects within 10 percent on capital cost and 48 percent within 20 percent on ridership | A pre-registered forecast, resolved and published by law | Tens of projects, a decade of lag, PDFs, no score |
| A published delivery-confidence rating on every major project | UK Infrastructure and Projects Authority, now NISTA, [annually since 2013](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/major-projects-data) | A dated, public forecast of delivery | Never scored against what followed; [`gmpp/`](../gmpp/results/report.md) scores it |
| Models of overrun from bidding-stage variables | [Moon et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1080/13467581.2020.1765171): 234 Korean public projects, 61 percent accuracy. [Jacques de Sousa et al. 2024](https://www.emerald.com/ecam/article/31/13/285-302/1232829): 4,772 Portuguese construction contracts, 85.5 percent accuracy, 68.3 percent once the publication-year feature is dropped | Predicts a real cost outcome before award | Retrospective, unregistered, one country each |
| Early-warning model on open contracting data | [Gallego, Rivero and Martínez 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7368425/): Colombia's SECOP, AUC 0.79 to 0.91 for fines, breach listings and contract extensions | Outcome prediction on public procurement records | Retrospective; the authors warn that award choices already reflect risk |
| Reference class forecasting | [Flyvbjerg 2006](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.3642), first applied for the UK Department for Transport in 2004; the Edinburgh Tram and Crossrail were [early cases](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:82761425-46df-4200-9ef1-b611d73433af) | A base-rate forecast of overrun at appraisal | We found no public release of the underlying project database, and no published scoring of the forecasts against outturns |
| Predicting World Bank project ratings | [Geli, Kraay and Nobakht 2014](https://ideas.repec.org/p/wbk/wbrwps/7001.html): early project characteristics beat staff self-ratings. [Jordan, Honig and Tsai](https://mitgovlab.org/events/evaluating-20000-world-bank-development-projects-can-machine-learning-help-predict-which-projects-will-succeed-or-fail/): embeddings of 20,000 project documents | Appraisal information to an independent outcome rating | No registration, and no public joined dataset until [`worldbank/`](../worldbank/results/report.md) |
| Vendor ratings tied to future awards | [Decarolis, Spagnolo and Pacini](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22814): at an Italian utility, supplier performance rose from 25 to 90 percent of audited parameters and held for ten years | Shows that scored outcomes change supplier behaviour | Proprietary data; ratings look backward |
| The US past-performance mandate | The overhauled [FAR 15.104(b)(3)](https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-15) keeps "Past performance must be evaluated in every competitive source selection" | Every competition already judges each offeror's record | Ratings are closed ([FAR 42.1503](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/42.1503)) and unscored; a 25-year retrospective on the reforms found it "[hard to say that the system's performance has improved](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/2017/12/reinventing-government-turns-25/357979/)" |
| A public vendor scoreboard | [Texas Vendor Performance Tracking System](https://comptroller.texas.gov/purchasing/programs/vendor-performance-tracking/): agencies grade vendors A to F; D and F grades wait 30 days for a vendor response | Shows public vendor grades are workable | Grades past work; forecasts nothing |
| Corruption and irregularity flags | [Open Contracting Partnership](https://www.open-contracting.org/resources/red-flags-in-public-procurement-a-guide-to-using-data-to-detect-and-mitigate-risks/) (73 indicators), [ChileCompra](https://www.open-contracting.org/2019/06/06/red-flags-in-chile/), Ukraine's [DOZORRO](https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/dozorro/) | Scores tenders on public data before award | Flags risk of misconduct, a different target from delivery; none publishes calibration |
| Opportunity scoring sold to bidders | For example [Deltek GovWin IQ](https://www.deltek.com/en/government-contracting/govwin), whose "Smart Fit Scores give visibility into your best-fit leads" | Commercial prediction inside procurement | Helps a contractor choose what to bid on, and says nothing about what happens after award |
| Schedule forecasting for project owners | [nPlan](https://www.nplan.io/): trained on 750,000 historical construction schedules; Network Rail and HS2 among its customers | Forecasts delivery outcomes commercially | Works on schedules already under contract; no published backtest |
| AI inside US source selection | The Army's experimental FAST TRACK tool is the subject of [TRAX International's Court of Federal Claims suit](https://www.defenseone.com/policy/2026/08/ai-army-contract-lawsuit/415261/) after [GAO denied the protest](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-424271,b-424271.2,b-424271.3); an [Army SBIR topic](https://armysbir.army.mil/topics/ai-enabled-source-selection-solution-for-contract-proposal-evaluation/) seeks evaluation software; a [DHS pilot](https://fedscoop.com/dhs-artificial-intelligence-past-performance-data-procurement-innovation-lab/) retrieved relevant past-performance records | Government is experimenting | Workflow and retrieval; the live legal question is documentation of what the tool contributed |
| Pre-registered metrics for procurement reform | [Madeline Hart, "Keeping Score on Procurement Reform"](https://www.defensetechacquisition.com/p/keeping-score-on-procurement-reform): four spending metrics and a stated success threshold for fiscal year 2026 | A public, checkable prediction about procurement | Aggregates for a reform agenda, where this project backtests forecasts on individual awards |

## How well ex-ante judgment predicts outcomes

Grantmaking has measured this more than procurement has. Within the fundable range, NIH percentile scores discriminate later productivity at AUC 0.54 across 102,740 grants ([Fang, Bowen and Casadevall 2016](https://elifesciences.org/articles/13323)), and reviewers given the same applications agree with each other at an intraclass correlation of zero ([Pier et al. 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5866547/)). Explicit forecasts do better: markets picked which studies would replicate 73 percent of the time across 103 findings ([Gordon et al. 2021](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0248780)). Across 53,298 forecasts of 100 social-science projects, raw forecasts underperformed a constant at the mean, and shrunk crowd forecasts beat it by about 10 percent ([DellaVigna and Vivalt 2026](https://sdellavi.com/pdf/DellaVignaVivaltSSPP_paper.pdf)). A procurement scoreboard should therefore publish base rates first and report skill above them.

## Public data that pairs award-time information with later outcomes

| Source | Known at award | Outcome and how it resolves | Are losing bids visible? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [USAspending](https://api.usaspending.gov/docs/endpoints) (US federal) | Contract type, competition, offers received, set-aside, product and industry codes, office, ceiling, planned dates | Each later action carries a reason-for-modification code, the change in ceiling, and the current end date, so ceiling growth, schedule slip and termination resolve from monthly archives. See [`usaspending/`](../usaspending/results/report.md) | No |
| [UK major projects data](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/major-projects-data) | Rating, description, baselines, dates | The next year's rating, whole-life cost and end date. See [`gmpp/`](../gmpp/results/report.md) | Not applicable |
| World Bank [documents API](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/api) and [IEG ratings](https://financesone.worldbank.org/ieg-world-bank-project-performance-ratings/DS00053) | Full appraisal document text | Independent outcome rating, keyed on the project number. See [`worldbank/`](../worldbank/results/report.md) | Not applicable |
| Ukraine [Prozorro](https://public-api.prozorro.gov.ua/api/2.5/tenders) | Every bidder's identity and price, tender terms, buyer | Typed contract changes in the registry, including duration extensions. See [`prozorro/`](../prozorro/results/report.md) | Yes |
| [AusTender](https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/19) and [Paraguay DNCP](https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/63) via the Open Contracting registry | Buyer, supplier, method, value | Contract amendments (Australia); payments and milestones (Paraguay) | Paraguay names tenderers |
| [SBIR.gov](https://www.sbir.gov/data-resources) | Phase I award abstracts | A later Phase II award | No |
| UK [contract performance notices](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-manage-phase/guidance-contract-performance-notices-html) | Contract and supplier | From 2026, contracts above £5 million get a published rating on a fixed four-point scale at least yearly | No |

US contractor performance ratings (CPARS) would be the richest label and are closed by regulation. Losing proposals stay confidential in most systems, so a forecast for a bid that lost never resolves; this project backtests forecasts for the awarded contract and tests bidder-level signal where bids are public.

## Rules for using a forecast inside source selection

In the United States, every factor that affects award must be stated in the solicitation ([FAR 15.304](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/15.304)), and an offeror with no record "may not be evaluated favorably or unfavorably on past performance" ([FAR 15.305](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/15.305)). The United Kingdom's guidance on AI in procurement asks authorities to check that tender commitments are deliverable and supplies no method ([PPN 017, summarised by Burges Salmon](https://www.burges-salmon.com/articles/102k15q/improving-transparency-of-ai-use-in-public-procurement-ppn-017-published/)). A scoreboard that runs outside the award process, on public records, touches none of these rules. Advisory use inside an evaluation needs disclosure in the solicitation and neutral treatment of new entrants.

## What we could not verify

- An Institute for Defense Analyses study reported to find that text models could not beat chance on defense program outcomes; we could not open the paper.
- Whether the historical feeds of the US Federal IT Dashboard, which paired risk ratings with realised variance, remain retrievable after its 2026 sunset.
- How often the FPDS solicitation identifier links an award to its SAM.gov solicitation; [`usaspending/`](../usaspending/results/report.md) measures the fill rate at 43 to 69 percent of base awards by year, and link quality beyond that is unmeasured.
